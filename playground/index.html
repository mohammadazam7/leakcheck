The model scored 99%. Did it see the answers?

A model is trained on one part of the data and scored on the rest. When the answer slips from one part into the other, the score looks excellent and means nothing. leakcheck finds where it slips through, before any model is trained.

The split to check

The files are read by this page and stay on your machine. Up to 25 MB each.

What the columns are

Who or what a row is about

Such as a customer or a patient: nobody should be on both sides.

    What it looks for
    
          
    
            
    
              
    A column that gives the answer away
    
              
    
                One column alone predicts the target almost perfectly on the test rows. Usually
                it is the outcome recorded another way, or filled in afterwards.
              
    
            
    
            
    
              
    Copied rows
    
              
    The same record, identical in every column, on both sides of the split.
    
            
    
            
    
              
    The same customer on both sides
    
              
    
                The model is tested on people it has already seen, so it can recognise them
                instead of learning anything general.
              
    
            
    
            
    
              
    The future in the training rows
    
              
    
                Training rows dated after the test period begins. In real use the model never
                gets to learn from tomorrow.
              
    
            
    
          
    
          
    
            A finding is a reason to look, not a verdict, and a split with no findings can still
            leak in ways one column at a time cannot show. The
            limits are in the README.
